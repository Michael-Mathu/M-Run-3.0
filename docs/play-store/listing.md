## Full description (max 4000)

```
Mwendo ("pace" in Swahili) is a running tracker inspired by East Africa's distance-running tradition. Record your runs with accurate GPS, race against the pace of record-setting performances, and learn something between runs.

TRACK EVERY RUN
• Run, walk, cycle, hike or drive, with live distance, pace, time and elevation
• A clean GPS line: bad fixes are filtered out, so no zig-zags and no phantom distance
• Keeps recording with the screen off, shown by an ongoing notification
• Voice cues every kilometre, in English or Kiswahili, so you never look at the screen
• Auto-pause at traffic lights, and calories based on your weight
• OpenStreetMap maps
• GPS status at a glance, so you know when it is safe to start

RACE THE LEGENDS
• Pick a famous performance, from 800 m to the marathon, and run against its pace as a ghost on your map
• Four difficulty levels, from a gentle Bronze target up to the real record pace
• See how far ahead or behind you are, split by split

LEARN AS YOU GO
• Mwendo Academy: short lessons on training, technique, recovery and the history of East African running
• The stories behind the athletes and their records

STAY MOTIVATED
• Weekly distance, streaks, challenges, XP and levels
• Your full activity history with route, pace and elevation
• Export an activity as GPX, or share a summary

YOUR DATA STAYS WITH YOU
• No account and no sign-up
• No ads, no analytics
• Runs are stored on your phone only, and you can delete any of them
• SOS button that prepares a message with your location for your emergency contacts

Available in English and Kiswahili, in light and dark mode.

Mwendo is an independent app and is not endorsed by or affiliated with any athlete. Athlete photos are freely licensed images from Wikimedia Commons, credited in the app.

Mwendo is a fitness tool, not a medical device. Talk to a doctor before starting a new training programme.
```
