# Mwendo Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 15 November 2026_

Mwendo is a running, walking and cycling tracker. This policy explains what
the Android app does with your information. The short version: **your runs
stay on your phone.** The app has no account, no ads and no analytics.

## What the app stores, and where

Everything below is stored **only on your device**, in the app's private
storage. It is not uploaded to us or to anyone else.

| Information | Why |
| :--- | :--- |
| Your location during a recording (GPS route, speed, altitude, time) | To draw your route and work out distance, pace and elevation |
| Your activities (date, type, distance, duration, calories, route) | Your activity history, statistics, challenges and levels |
| The display name and profile photo you choose | Shown on the You tab |
| Emergency contacts you add (name, phone number) | The SOS button |
| App settings (units, language, theme, colour) | To remember your preferences |

Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data in Android settings, deletes all
of it. You can also delete individual activities inside the app.

## Location

Mwendo asks for **precise location while the app is in use**. Location is
read only:

- while the Run tab is open, to show where you are on the map; and
- while you are recording an activity. Recording continues with the screen
  off or the app in the background, and Android shows an ongoing
  "Mwendo is tracking your run" notification the whole time. Recording stops
  when you stop it.

Mwendo does not ask for "Allow all the time" background location and never
reads your location when you are not using it.

## What leaves your device

- **Map tiles.** The map is OpenStreetMap data served by
  [OpenFreeMap](https://openfreemap.org). To draw the map, the app downloads
  the map images for the area on screen. Like any website request, this
  reveals your IP address and the area being viewed to OpenFreeMap. No
  account, name, route or activity is sent.
- **SOS.** If you press SOS, Mwendo opens your phone's own messaging app
  with a message to your emergency contacts containing a link to your current
  position. Nothing is sent unless you press Send in that app.
- **Sharing.** If you choose to share or export an activity (for example a
  GPX file or a summary), it goes to the app you pick in Android's share
  sheet.
- **Links.** Photo-credit links open in your web browser.

Nothing else is transmitted. Mwendo contains no advertising, analytics or
crash-reporting software, and we do not sell or share personal information.

## Permissions

| Permission | Use |
| :--- | :--- |
| Location (precise, while in use) | Recording activities and showing your position |
| Foreground service (location) | Keeps a recording running with the screen off |
| Notifications | The ongoing recording notification |
| Camera / photo picker (only when you choose a profile photo) | Your profile photo, which stays on your device |
| Internet | Downloading map tiles |

## Photos of athletes

The portraits of athletes in the Legends section are freely licensed images
from Wikimedia Commons, credited inside the app (You → Settings → Photo
credits). Mwendo is not endorsed by or affiliated with any athlete shown.

## Children

Mwendo is not directed at children under 13.
